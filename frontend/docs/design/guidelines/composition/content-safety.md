# Content and Decision Safety

## Purpose

Rumper supports consequential property decisions. Composition must help buyers
understand evidence and uncertainty without creating false confidence or fear.

## Required information model

Every significant finding must keep these elements distinct:

```text
Source evidence
Rumper interpretation
Confidence
Limitations
Recommended next action
```

## AI assistant boundaries

The assistant may:

- explain findings in plain language;
- answer questions about available evidence;
- suggest questions for expert review;
- summarize already visible information.

The assistant must not:

- present simulated progress as actual verified analysis;
- claim sources were retrieved when they were not;
- replace cited evidence;
- guarantee safety or suitability;
- hide uncertainty behind confident language.

Label AI-generated summaries and activity logs explicitly.

## Score and risk presentation

- A score is a summary, not proof.
- Explain what contributes to the score.
- State confidence and limitations.
- Use neutral score visualization and a labeled semantic status.
- Do not use bright brand green for score rings or risk conclusions.
- Do not describe a property as safe, suitable, or risk-free.

## Map safety

- State the source, date, scale, and precision of map layers.
- Distinguish exact, approximate, and inferred boundaries.
- Provide text alternatives for spatial findings.
- Do not imply causation or certainty from visual proximity alone.

## Expert review and sharing

- Explain what expert review covers and does not cover.
- Sharing a report must preserve status, limitations, and source references.
- Do not let shared previews remove uncertainty or preliminary labels.

## Content order

Recommended order:

1. Decision summary
2. Important limitations
3. Top findings
4. Supporting evidence and confidence
5. Recommended next actions
6. Detailed methodology

## Language

- Use calm, direct Bahasa Indonesia for customer-facing content.
- Prefer "Perlu validasi lebih lanjut" over absolute warnings.
- Prefer "Bukti mendukung..." over "Aman."
- Prefer "Risiko teridentifikasi..." over alarmist language.
- Use unambiguous dates such as `13 Jun 2026`.

## Public and sample data

Use fictional or anonymized property, customer, and evidence data in public
examples and prototypes.

## Correct examples

- "Risiko sedang berdasarkan lima modul; data drainase terbaru belum tersedia."
- "Ringkasan AI berdasarkan bukti yang ditampilkan. Belum ditinjau ahli."
- "Area berwarna menunjukkan batas perkiraan dari sumber regional."

## Incorrect examples

- "Analisis selesai. Properti ini aman."
- "AI menemukan semua risiko tersembunyi."
- A green score ring with no limitations or confidence explanation.

## Rules

- Protect, do not frighten.
- Never guarantee safety or suitability.
- Keep limitations visible.
- Label preliminary, incomplete, inferred, AI-generated, reviewed, and verified
  content.
- Never hide safety-critical information behind payment or optional disclosure.
