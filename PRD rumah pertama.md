## **1. Product Overview** 

## **Product Name** 

**Location Risk Advisor for First-Time Home Buyers** 

## **Product Summary** 

Location Risk Advisor is a web-based decision-support platform designed to help first-time home buyers in Greater Jakarta (Jabodetabek) evaluate the risks and suitability of residential locations before purchasing a home. 

Unlike traditional property listing platforms that primarily focus on price, property specifications, and promotional content, this product focuses on helping users understand the real-world risks and longterm livability of a location. 

The platform provides simplified, actionable insights related to: 

- Flood risk 

- Daily mobility and commute burden 

- Transportation accessibility 

- Nearby facilities and services 

- Environmental quality 

- Road and physical access conditions 

- Area development prospects 

- Long-term residential suitability 

The goal is to help users make more confident and informed housing decisions before committing to booking fees or down payments. 

## **2. Problem Statement** 

First-time home buyers often make high-stakes purchasing decisions without having sufficient visibility into the actual risks and quality of a residential area. 

1 

Most existing property platforms emphasize: 

- Property pricing 

- House photos 

- Building specifications 

- Developer promotions 

However, they provide limited support in evaluating critical location-related risks such as: 

- Flood exposure 

- Traffic congestion 

- Transportation access 

- Environmental conditions 

- Infrastructure quality 

- Accessibility to daily facilities 

- Long-term neighborhood suitability 

As a result, buyers may unknowingly choose locations that negatively affect their daily lives, commute experience, family needs, or long-term quality of living. 

## **3. Product Goals** 

## **Primary Goal** 

Help first-time home buyers clearly understand and evaluate location risks before purchasing a property. 

## **Key Objectives** 

1. Help users identify residential areas that match their lifestyle and budget 

2. Help users compare multiple locations objectively 

3. Reduce the risk of choosing unsuitable or high-risk locations 

4. Simplify complex geographic and infrastructure data into understandable insights 

5. Improve buyer confidence during the property evaluation process

## **14. Product Risks** 

## **1. Data Accuracy Risk** 

## **Risk** 

Incorrect or outdated data may reduce trust. 

## **Mitigation** 

- Confidence score indicators 

- Source transparency 

- Clear disclaimers 

- Encourage field validation 

9 

## **2. Product Complexity Risk** 

## **Risk** 

Too much information may overwhelm users. 

## **Mitigation** 

- Simplified scoring 

- Layered information architecture • Guided workflows 

- Progressive disclosure 

## **3. Recommendation Bias Risk** 

## **Risk** 

Users may perceive favoritism toward certain developers or areas. 

## **Mitigation** 

- Transparent scoring logic 

- Balanced pros and cons 

- Separation between data and recommendations 

## **4. Over-Reliance Risk** 

## **Risk** 

Users may depend entirely on the platform without conducting site visits. 

## **Mitigation** 

- Strong survey recommendations 

- Validation checklists 

- Confidence indicators 

- Resident interview prompts
