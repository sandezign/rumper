# Target Persona

## Primary Persona: "Si Commuter" & "Si Perencana"
- **Demographics**: Young professionals / young couples in Jabodetabek. First-time home buyers.
- **Context**: They have just completed the onboarding wizard and are waiting for, or viewing, their property's risk report.
- **Motivation**: To clearly see the red flags and understand the score so they can make a go/no-go decision on the property, verify if the property is safe from floods and close to work.
- **Current Workaround**: Looking at separate Google Maps tabs, scrolling through PDF reports, reading local news for floods, asking neighbors.
- **Pain Point**: Trusting an AI-generated property score without seeing the raw geospatial evidence or knowing what to verify on-site.
